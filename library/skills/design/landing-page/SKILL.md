# Landing Page

People decide in five seconds. Lead with the outcome, prove it, then explain.

## Structure
- Hero: outcome-focused headline (≤ 10 words), one-line subhead, one primary CTA, a real screenshot.
- Proof: numbers, logos or a short demo.
- Features as benefits with visuals; then how it works; FAQ; final CTA.

## Copy
- Concrete over clever; numbers over adjectives; the reader's words, not internal jargon.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "next-gen"); show it instead.
