# Postmortem

Fix the system, not the person.

## Template
- Summary and customer impact (who, how long, how bad).
- Timeline with timestamps from detection to resolution.
- Root cause and contributing factors (5 whys).
- What went well / what went poorly.
- Action items with owners and due dates — prefer prevention over detection over mitigation.
